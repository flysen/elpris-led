Technical details

The board's blue LED

From v5.0 the small blue LED on the ESP32 board shows what the lamp is doing, including its network address after it starts. The default settings below are the lamp's; the others are for comparison. It can be turned off in the lamp's web page.

Longer is easier to count, shorter is quicker to show. The lamp uses 250 ms.

Experiments: other color scales

Ideas for other ways to color the strip, for comparison with Cheapest to most expensive. These are not in the lamp.

Elpriser tillhandahålls av Elpriset just nu.se. The real prices are fetched from there every hour. They are spot prices excluding VAT, grid fees and tax, so your electricity bill is higher.

The emulator and the Compared with a normal price color mode were designed by @sealjo. The example days are made up but have a realistic shape. The emulator always counts 96 quarters per day; on the two days a year when the clocks change, the lamp handles the 23- or 25-hour day correctly but the emulator doesn't.
