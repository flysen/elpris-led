Elpris-LED emulator

Runs the color logic of Elpris-LED v4 in the browser. The top strip is the default color mode, Compared with a normal price. Below it is the other mode, Cheapest to most expensive, and optionally a third strip with free variants of it to experiment with. Install page · GitHub

Time and prices

 

The lamp starts looking for tomorrow's prices at 13:30. When they arrive, the colors are recalculated.

The strip (the lamp's settings)

The lamp's default is 100. With 96 or 192, every LED gets whole quarters.

Compared with a normal price

Today fills the strip from midnight to midnight, colored by how the price compares with a normal price. Yellow means it doesn't matter. After one off LED come two LEDs comparing tomorrow's average 08–17 and 17–24 with the same hours today. They are off until tomorrow's prices are published, which the lamp notices from 13:30.

The average of these days plus today and, if ticked below, tomorrow.

In öre from the normal price. A price of zero or less is always very cheap. The same limits apply to the two tomorrow LEDs, measured as the difference from today. Only used with 5 levels.

Variants of the algorithm (experiments)

Ideas for other scales, for comparison. These are not in the lamp.

Emulator and the normal-price color mode designed by @sealjo. The example days are made up but have a realistic shape. The emulator counts 96 quarters per day and ignores the 23- and 25-hour days at the daylight saving time changes; the lamp handles them. Colors follow the FastLED rainbow color wheel that the lamp uses.
